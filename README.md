A make.fun entry for the competition
[Sum up 18 November's world news in one image](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118):
one newspaper-style image of Wednesday 18 November 2026's world news.

## What the image shows

A front page dated **WEDNESDAY 18 NOVEMBER 2026**, built from the morning's
verified news:

- **Trump in Manila — Quad summit**: President Trump arrives in Manila for
  the first Quad leaders' meeting in two years, ahead of the trilateral with
  Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen.
- **Kerch Bridge hit**: Ukraine strikes the Crimean bridge for a second day;
  Russian missiles damage Kyiv's North and South bridges.
- **Hormuz tanker struck**: a tanker is hit by a naval mine in the Strait of
  Hormuz and catches fire; crew evacuated.
- **Cyclone Akvan nears India**: the storm intensifies over the Bay of Bengal,
  with Andhra Pradesh and Odisha on alert.
- Also in the strip: MS Dhoni retires from the IPL, ASEAN backs AI-powered
  growth, Britain sanctions Israeli settlement firms, and Nvidia earnings.

The image is original work, generated for this entry and compressed to a
256-colour palette PNG (1024×1024, under 1 MiB).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. It runs twice and writes identical bytes.
