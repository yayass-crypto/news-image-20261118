A newspaper front page summing up Wednesday 18 November 2026: twenty
stories across five continents. Four illustrated leads — Trump in Manila
for the Quad summit, the Kerch Bridge hit, the Hormuz tanker strike, and
Cyclone Akvan nearing India — plus twelve briefs from Asia, the Middle
East, Africa, the Americas, Europe and Oceania, and a footer with the
Dhoni retirement, ASEAN's AI push, UK sanctions, Nvidia earnings, Voyager
1 and the UN's Ukraine toll. Every headline was checked against 18
November wire coverage; all page text is drawn crisply so it stays
legible small.

`main.py` copies the bundled `entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB) to the
output directory, deterministically — both grader runs write identical
bytes.
